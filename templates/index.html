Scan documents for privacy risk

Upload one or more files to detect emails, phone numbers, SSNs, credit card numbers, API keys, addresses, and more — scored on a single risk dashboard.

What gets detected

  • Emails, phone numbers, physical addresses
  • SSNs, passport & driver's license numbers
  • Credit cards (Luhn-validated), IBANs, bank accounts
  • AWS keys, API keys/secrets, private key blocks
  • Dates of birth, medical record references

How scoring works

Each finding is weighted by severity (low → critical) and combined into a 0–100 risk score with diminishing returns, so one critical leak stands out without a large document maxing out unfairly.

Privacy of the scan itself

Files are parsed in-memory, scanned, then immediately deleted from disk. Only the redacted findings (never full matches) are shown and kept for this session.
